 v2.0 — Basic Product/UI Understanding Document
Purpose: This document explains what pages TARANG will have, what each page contains, who can access it, and
what the user can actually do on that page. It is intentionally different from the formal SRS.
Architecture: 31 pages across Public, Authentication, Core Application, Administration and Shared areas.

Page 2
1. How to Read This Document
Think of this PDF as the blueprint / table-of-contents guide for the TARANG website and platform. It is not a
technical requirements specification. The goal is to give the team a quick picture of the complete product before
development starts.
For every page, we define:
 Page name and URL
 Who can access it
 Why the page exists
 Main content/components shown
 What the user can do
 What happens after an important action
Overall product flow: Public awareness → Authentication → Survey upload → SSS preprocessing → AI detection
→ XAI/physics validation → Geotagging → Duplicate suppression → Expert review → Risk assessment → Cleanup
planning → Mission execution → Impact/reporting → Retraining.

4. What Happens Behind the Pages — Core Platform Flow
The pages are not isolated screens. They form one operational workflow:
Stage
Main page
What happens
1. Ingest
Upload Survey
Files + metadata enter TARANG; originals remain traceable.
2. Prepare
Processing Status
Formats are standardized, synchronized and preprocessed.
3. Detect
AI Analysis
AI generates detection/segmentation results and confidence.
4. Explain & Validate
AI Analysis / Review
XAI and sonar-physics evidence support interpretation.
5. Locate
Map / Target Registry
Detections receive geospatial coordinates and uncertainty.
6. Fuse
Target Registry
Repeated observations are associated into unique physical targets.
7. Verify
Marine Review Portal
Experts accept, reject, correct or mark unknown.
8. Prioritize
Hotspots / Impact
Risk and spatial analytics support decisions.
9. Act
Mission Planner
Verified targets become cleanup missions.
10. Measure
Impact / Reports
Recovery and impact are recorded and reported.
11. Learn
Retraining
Expert feedback becomes controlled training data for future models.

6. One-Page Mental Model of TARANG
PUBLIC → Understand TARANG, view safe validated information, report observations.
OPERATORS → Upload and monitor sonar surveys.
AI ENGINE → Preprocess → detect → segment → explain → physics-check → geotag → associate duplicates.
MARINE EXPERTS → Validate AI results and create trusted targets.
AUTHORITIES → See risks, hotspots, trends and reports.
NGOs → Convert verified targets into cleanup missions and record recovery.
RESEARCHERS → Analyze anonymized historical data.
ADMIN → Govern users, organizations, security, logs and model lifecycle.
LEARNING LOOP → Expert feedback → curated dataset → retraining → evaluation → controlled deployment →
monitoring.
This blueprint is intended to give the UI, frontend, backend, AI and system-research teams the same understanding of
what each page is for and what it needs to accomplish before detailed implementation.
